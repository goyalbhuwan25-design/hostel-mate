About

HostelMate

HostelMate is a student-focused expense management application designed to make shared hostel expenses simple, transparent and organized.

Project Purpose

We built HostelMate to help roommates track their daily hostel costs, split shared expenses fairly, and settle balances without confusion.

Features

  • Expense tracking and categorization
  • Smart splitting between roommates
  • Settlement and balance management
  • Monthly and category analytics
  • Responsive, local-first experience

Technology Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • LocalStorage
  • Chart.js
